Nursultan / Fashion technology

PERLA

PERLA helps shoppers decide whether a look suits them before they buy it. A virtual fitting room lets them explore different looks online. Brands can use interest in those looks to understand which styles shoppers want.

From fitting room to feedback

PERLA explores a question that matters to both sides of a fashion purchase. A shopper wants to know how a look might suit them, while a brand wants to know which looks are attracting attention. The prototype connects those activities through a virtual fitting room and a view of shopper interest. The project film demonstrates the experience, and the repository documents the shopper and brand features.

Technologies

React · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS
