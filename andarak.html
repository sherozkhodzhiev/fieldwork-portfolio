Sherozbek / Village archive

Andarak

Andarak brings the village’s history together in a bilingual archive. Readers can follow a historical chapter or browse photographs and listen to its accompanying music. The two languages give more families a way to explore and share the same stories.

Exploring the archive

A visitor can follow the village’s history chapter by chapter, with music accompanying the photographs, or open the gallery to explore the images independently. A weather forecast brings present-day Andarak into an archive focused on its past. The project guide in the repository explains how to edit the chapters and how the React front end fits together with the FastAPI service.

Technologies

React · FastAPI · MongoDB
